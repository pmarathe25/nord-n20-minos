# Reports

Dated measurements, benchmark findings and other temporary investigations belong here. Keep durable architecture, operating instructions and configuration guidance in `docs/`, and reusable measurement tools in `tools/`.

Commit concise reports with their methods, versions and limitations. Keep raw logs, downloaded binaries, disposable worlds and scratch results outside Git; delete them after reviewing and recording the findings. `reports/raw/` and `local/` are ignored scratch locations.
